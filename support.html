Subscriptions

  • Restore purchases: Open the app → Settings → Restore Purchases (if available).
  • Manage / cancel: iPhone Settings → your Apple ID → Subscriptions.
  • Trial not showing? Trials are available once per subscription group per Apple ID.

Data & privacy

  • Delete my data: email support with the subject Delete my account.
  • Export my data: email support with the subject Export my data.

Common troubleshooting

  • If sync looks stuck, try closing and reopening the app.
  • If something looks wrong, email support with a screenshot if you can.
