What this policy covers

This policy explains how we collect, use, store, and share personal data when you use the farrier app mobile app (iOS/Android).

Personal data we process

Depending on how you use the app, we may process the following categories of data:

  • Account data: email address and authentication identifiers (used to sign you in).
  • Client data you enter: client names, addresses, postcodes, notes and other details you choose to store.
  • Location / geocoding data: location coordinates derived from client addresses or locations you store for route planning.
  • Photos: images you upload (e.g. horse photos) and any captions/notes you attach.
  • Distance / routing data: calculated distances and travel-time estimates between client locations.
  • Subscription status: information about whether you have an active subscription (if you purchase one).

What we do not collect

  • No analytics / advertising tracking: we do not run third-party analytics or ad tracking SDKs in the app.
  • No card details: we do not collect or store your payment card details (payments are handled by Apple/Google).
  • No device GPS navigation from current location: we do not require or use your device’s current GPS location to provide route planning.

How we use your data

  • To provide core features (client management, planning, scheduling, route/distance calculations).
  • To store and sync your data across devices (if sync is enabled).
  • To maintain security, prevent abuse, and troubleshoot issues.
  • To respond to support requests.
  • To confirm subscription access (if you subscribe).

Legal basis (UK GDPR)

We process personal data under one or more of these bases, depending on context:

  • Contract: to provide the service you request when using the app.
  • Legitimate interests: to keep the service secure and reliable.
  • Consent: where required (for example, if you enable optional device permissions).

Service providers (processors) and sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We share data only as needed to run the app:

Supabase (authentication, database, file storage)

We use Supabase to provide user authentication (Supabase Auth), store your app data (database), and store uploaded images (Supabase Storage). This means your account data, client records, locations, and photos may be stored and processed by Supabase on our behalf.

Google Maps Platform (mapping / geocoding / distance calculations)

We use the Google Maps Platform APIs to support mapping and location features (for example, converting addresses into coordinates and calculating distances/travel times between locations).

When you use these features, relevant location information (such as client addresses, postcodes, or coordinates) may be sent to Google to provide the requested functionality. Google acts as an independent provider of these API services.

Payments and subscriptions (Apple / Google)

If you purchase a subscription, payment is processed by the Apple App Store (iOS) and/or Google Play (Android). We do not receive your full payment details. We may receive information necessary to confirm subscription access (such as whether a subscription is active).

International transfers

Our service providers may process data on servers located outside your country. Where international transfers occur, we rely on appropriate safeguards as required by applicable data protection law.

Retention

  • We keep your data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the service.
  • If you delete your account, we will delete or anonymise your personal data within a reasonable period, except where we must keep certain data for legal, security, or operational reasons.

Your rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Object to or restrict certain processing
  • Request a copy of your data (data portability), where applicable

To exercise these rights, contact us at simongarielfleet@gamil.com. If you are in the UK, you can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO).

Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your data. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we work to protect your information.

Children

farrier app is not intended for children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will update the “Last updated” date above and, where appropriate, provide additional notice in the app.
